# Regulatory and legal launch gate (US/California)

**This is an issues list, not a legal conclusion or exemption determination.** RomaSwap must obtain counsel's written evaluation of actual deployed functions and supported jurisdictions before live trading.

1. **SEC:** Evaluate broker-dealer, exchange and adviser functions for any swaps involving crypto asset securities. Check current SEC staff positions about self-custodial swap interfaces and eligibility conditions; staff statements may be nonbinding and conditional.
2. **FinCEN:** Analyze whether RomaSwap merely publishes software or conducts money-transmission/exchange activity, including routing and fee settlement specifics. Noncustodial branding does not decide classification.
3. **California DFAL/DFPI:** Determine licensing scope, relevant statutory exemptions for software/decentralized connectivity, revenue-based exceptions if applicable, and go-live restrictions. The July 2026 regulatory framework requires current verification.
4. **OFAC/sanctions:** Risk-based wallet screening and blocked activity handling, consistent with applicable obligations, with counsel-reviewed policies. Consider provider terms and geographic restrictions.
5. **Consumer disclosures:** Clear fee, quote, slippage, wrapping/token, routing, privacy, and loss-risk disclosures; terms of service and compliance procedures.
6. **Token listing:** Counsel and security approve allowed contracts/jurisdictions before tokens appear. Distinguish native BTC from tokenized cbBTC, no implied redemption guarantee.
7. **Fees/taxes:** Legal entity, revenue receipts, tax basis, USDC issuer/depeg risks, compliance record retention and treasury handling.

**Launch gate:** no public live swaps or customer fee collection without written attorney review of SEC, FinCEN, state law, OFAC/control obligations; validated token policy; security review; and explicit owner signoff.
